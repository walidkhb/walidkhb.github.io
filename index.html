Signature Programmes

Platform Strategy 50+ systems

Integration-as-a-Platform Strategy

Integration moves from a cost centre to the bank’s operating backbone.

What I set in motion: Repositioned integration as a funded, owned platform with its own roadmap, team of 40+ and governance—replacing hundreds of one-off connections across 50+ systems, with every migration at zero downtime.

What it unlocks: Time-to-market down 70%. Partner onboarding in days, not months. Each new product makes the platform more valuable instead of more complex.

Cloud-Native Banking Platform

A bank that can launch and scale digital services independently.

What it unlocks: Digital products start on a platform where security, scaling and deployment are already solved—launched in weeks rather than months.

Platform Automation Fully automated

Zero-Touch Integration Platform

Change reaches production through the platform—never by hand, never by a vendor’s tooling.

What I set in motion: Moved the integration estate onto IBM Cloud Pak for Integration with GitOps: every release versioned, every change compliance-checked, API publishing automated. Made it policy that every vendor delivers through the bank’s own pipeline.

What it unlocks: Delivery no longer depends on individuals or suppliers. Audit, operations and vendors read one record of what changed, when and why.

Architecture Governance 10 standards

Enterprise Integration Framework & Standards

The bank sets the rules once; every project and supplier inherits them.

What I set in motion: Authored the bank’s Integration Framework: ten standards from principles and security to testing and observability, a four-gate review model and recorded architecture decisions—mandatory for every project and annexed to every technology RFP.

What it unlocks: Integration quality no longer depends on who delivers. Sourcing, vendor management and architecture all speak the same language.

AI-Native Transformation AI-native

Claude-Native Enterprise Enablement

AI as part of how the bank works every day—governed, not experimental.

What I set in motion: Introduced an AI-native delivery model built on Claude: engineers working with persistent project memory, an institutional-memory engine mapping programmes, changes, systems and obligations across the estate, and in-house tools running in production.

Regulatory Strategy 12+ mandates

Regulatory Connectivity Programme

Compliance as a capability the bank owns, not a crisis it reacts to.

What I set in motion: Led integration into SAMA’s national enforcement programme (Tanfeeth) across successive waves with round-the-clock automated processing, plus supervisory returns, regulatory and Open Banking reporting, PDPL consent and sanctions screening—12+ regulatory and partner programmes on one platform.

What it unlocks: The next mandate lands on known rails. The bank answers its regulator in real time and absorbs new rules at a fraction of the cost.

The bank of the next decade runs on three capabilities. I have built all three in production.
