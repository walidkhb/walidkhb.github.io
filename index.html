Flagship Programmes

Three programmes that define what the bank can do next.

Each one is bank-wide, runs in production, and was built to be extended—not finished.

1 platform
Enterprise Integration Platform

Moved the bank’s integration backbone from a legacy ESB to a cloud-native integration platform on Kubernetes—governed APIs, event streaming and messaging in one estate. Core banking, instant payments, SARIE, SADAD, channels, partners and regulators now connect through a single controlled layer instead of hundreds of point-to-point links.

Future value: every new product, fintech partner or mandate plugs into an existing capability. Integration cost falls with each project instead of rising.

AI-native
Claude-Native Engineering Enablement

Introduced an AI-native delivery model built on Claude. Engineers work alongside Claude with persistent project memory; an institutional-memory engine continuously maps programmes, change requests, systems and owners across the whole integration estate; and internal tools—such as a regulated-operations portal with SQL approval workflows and tiered roles—reach production in weeks, not quarters.

Future value: knowledge stops leaving with people, leaders see the full portfolio in one view, and the bank has a governed path to agentic AI on top of its platform.

Real-time
Regulatory Compliance at Scale

Led the bank’s integration into SAMA’s national enforcement programme (Tanfeeth) across successive waves—round-the-clock automated handling of government holds, transfers and account inquiries—alongside supervisory data returns, regulatory API and Open Banking reporting, PDPL consent services and sanctions screening.

Future value: compliance becomes a platform capability. The next mandate is a new integration on known rails, not a new crisis programme.

Where Banking Is Going

The bank of the next decade runs on three capabilities. I have built all three in production.

A platform every system speaks through.

Banks that compete on speed will not rebuild integrations per product. They will compose new offerings from governed APIs and events in days.

A live connection to the regulator.

Supervision is moving from periodic reports to real-time data and instructions. Banks that treat compliance as infrastructure will absorb each new mandate at a fraction of the cost.

AI that acts on institutional memory—under control.

The value of AI in banking comes when agents can see the estate, use governed APIs, and leave an audit trail. That requires the platform and the governance to exist first.
