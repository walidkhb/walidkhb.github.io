Platform Strategy 129 services in 2026

Integration-as-a-Platform Strategy

Integration moves from a cost centre to the bank’s operating backbone.

What I set in motion: Repositioned integration as a funded, owned platform with its own roadmap, team and governance—replacing one-off connections across 50+ systems, with every migration at zero downtime. In 2026 it carried 129 services into production (89 new), 392 releases, 49 new API products and four flagship launches: corporate internet banking, the IVR revamp, a digital partner app and national enforcement services.

What it unlocks: Time-to-market down 70%. Partner onboarding in days, not months. Each new product makes the platform more valuable instead of more complex.

Cloud-Native Architecture Active-active

Cloud-Native Banking Platform

A bank that can launch and scale digital services independently.

What I set in motion: Set the direction for containerised microservices on OpenShift, secured service-to-service with a zero-trust service mesh. Built resilience in by design: instant-payment checks run active-active across two sites, enforcement services across two data centres, card one-time passwords no longer depend on core banking, and notifications are served from both sites. Moved operations to a managed-services model with a 50-session knowledge-transfer programme so the know-how stays with the bank.

What it unlocks: A single site failure no longer stops critical services, and new digital products start on a platform where security, scaling and deployment are already solved.

Platform Automation 142 repositories

Automated Integration Delivery Platform

Change moves through the platform—versioned, checked and reversible—not by hand or by a vendor’s tooling.

What I set in motion: Put IBM Cloud Pak for Integration on CI/CD and GitOps: automated build and release for 12 integration platforms on a new engineering platform of 142 repositories, about two minutes from merge to a running build, the same checked package promoted through every environment, and 278 API products brought under Git control. Production stays gated by approval. Made it policy that every vendor delivers through the bank’s own pipeline.

What it unlocks: Delivery no longer depends on individuals or suppliers. Audit, operations and vendors read one record of what changed, when and why.
