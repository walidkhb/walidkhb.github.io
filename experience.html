Experience

From software engineer to vice president.

What changed at each step

Stage 4 · 2025–presentEnterprise technology influence

Jun 2025 – present

The Saudi Investment Bank · Riyadh

VP, Enterprise Integration

Head of Integration.

Responsibility
The bank’s integration strategy, platforms, engineering and delivery.
Scope
An organisation of 40+, running the integration platform that the bank’s channels, payment rails, regulators and partners depend on. New products and partners build on it instead of on bespoke integration.
Decision authority
Integration architecture and standards for the bank and its vendors. Made delivery through the bank’s own source control, CI/CD and deployment pipeline a requirement in integration modernisation sourcing.
Impact
  • Flagship programmes delivered in 2026: corporate internet banking, the IVR revamp, a digital partner app and national enforcement services.
  • The bank’s digital estate runs on the cloud-native platform I set the direction for in 2021. Built on it under my leadership: the SAIB Travel app, the new retail mobile app, the employee app and the corporate mobile app, along with enterprise-level digital services such as B2B corporate connectivity and SAMA’s national enforcement (Tanfeeth) services.
  • Retired legacy integration platforms, right-sized platform licences and improved cost and performance with a third-party service provider.
  • Took the managed-services model for integration from evaluation to implementation: scope of work, service levels, resource selection and knowledge transfer.
  • Led an architecture and security review of the corporate internet banking estate, with a prioritised remediation plan for executive management.
Leadership maturity
Influence beyond the function: contributed the integration and architecture position to strategic reviews of core banking modernisation, the treasury platform, corporate internet banking, the payment hub and Open Banking, and participate in the managed-services steering committee.

Programmes as Head of Integration, 2023 to date

  • Integration Framework & Standards: authored a single framework of standards and review gates, with threat modelling at design; the same gates apply to vendors.
  • Open Banking, built in-house: led the build-not-buy decision and the delivery of the bank’s own platform.
  • Payment hub integration: led the integration layer between the new payment hub and core banking for instant payments, SARIE, SADAD, bulk payments and request-to-pay.
  • Automated integration delivery: every change automated, audited and reversible, so delivery no longer depends on individuals or suppliers; production deployment stays approval-gated by design.
  • Regulatory connectivity: regulatory and partner programmes on one shared capability, including integration for SAMA’s national enforcement programme, so new mandates land on rails that already exist.
  • AI readiness: built the governed foundations AI needs and called for a bank-wide policy on AI-assisted engineering.

The turn from engineering delivery to leadership

Until 2019 my work was measured by what I built. From then on it was measured by what others could build, and whether the practices outlasted the programme.

Integration manager was my first management role. The Digital Factory was the first unit I founded, and its practices became the standard for new development.
