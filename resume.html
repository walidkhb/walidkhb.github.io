01 Executive profile

I am an enterprise technology and transformation executive in regulated banking, with 14+ years in enterprise technology, most of them in Saudi banking and government. I connect business strategy with engineering execution, turning fragmented technology delivery into governed, resilient, reusable capability that the institution keeps.

At The Saudi Investment Bank I reached vice president in seven years (2018–2025), moving from integration engineering to architecture leadership and then to running the function that the bank’s channels, payment rails, regulators and partners depend on. I now lead an organisation of 40+ that builds and runs the integration and cloud-native platforms the bank’s digital channels, partner services and regulatory connectivity are built on. My contribution is that capability to deliver, not any single project delivered on it.

Beyond my own function, I contribute the integration and architecture position to the bank’s strategic reviews, shape how integration is sourced, and set the standards that the bank and its vendors build to.

Value to the organisation

  • SpeedNew products and partners build on a shared, governed platform instead of bespoke integration.
  • ControlEvery change is automated, audited and reversible; delivery no longer depends on individuals or suppliers.
  • OwnershipStandards, pipelines and know-how owned by the bank, not rented from vendors.
  • ResilienceCritical payment services keep running through a site failure.
  • ReadinessNew regulatory mandates land on rails that already exist.

04 Platforms and capabilities

The platforms and capabilities I built, what each lets the bank do, and my part in it. Each title opens the full case study.

Personally ledSpeed to market

Integration as a platform

Repositioned integration as an owned platform, with its own roadmap, team and governance, across the bank’s core, channel, payment, partner and regulatory systems. It shortened time-to-market for new products and partners and made partner onboarding faster and repeatable.

Team-led deliveryControl by design

Automated integration delivery

Automated build and release across the bank’s integration platforms, with API products under Git control: every change is automated, audited and reversible, and delivery no longer depends on individuals or suppliers. Production releases are approval-gated by design.

Personally designedSame gates for vendors

Integration Framework

Authored the bank’s Integration Framework: one set of standards and review gates, with threat modelling at design and recorded architecture decisions. The same gates apply to vendors, so quality no longer depends on who delivers.

Personally ledBuilt in-house

Open Banking, built in-house

Led the build-not-buy decision and the delivery of the bank’s own Open Banking platform: account information and payment initiation services built to the FAPI 2.0 security profile.

Team-led deliveryAll payment rails

Payment and core banking API layers

One retry-safe path into core banking for every payment rail, from instant payments to SARIE, SADAD, bulk payments and request-to-pay. Governed business APIs and a simulator in place of direct access to the core, so projects test in parallel.

Team-led deliveryRegulatory readiness

Regulatory connectivity platform

Regulatory and partner connectivity built once as a shared platform, with known patterns for onboarding, security, monitoring and audit evidence, so new supervisory, credit-bureau and partner mandates land on rails that already exist.

Personally ledDigital channels

Resilient cloud-native platform

Set the direction for containerised microservices on OpenShift with a service mesh, beginning with the Digital Factory’s first microservices application. Under my leadership, the bank’s digital channels for retail, corporate and employee customers, and its enterprise, partner and B2B services, were built on it.

Contributed / proposedGoverned foundations

AI readiness

Built the governed foundations AI needs (compliance gates on every change, secured APIs), contributed the integration view to the bank’s secure-by-design review for AI enablement and called for a bank-wide policy on AI-assisted engineering. Personal Claude-native engineering practice.
