01 Executive profile

I am an enterprise technology and transformation executive in regulated banking, with 14+ years in enterprise technology, most of them in Saudi banking and government. I connect business strategy with engineering execution, turning fragmented technology delivery into governed, resilient, reusable capability that the institution keeps.

At The Saudi Investment Bank I reached vice president in seven years (2018–2025), moving from integration engineering to architecture leadership and then to running the function that the bank’s channels, payment rails, regulators and partners depend on. I now lead an organisation of 40+. In 2026 it put 129 integration services into production, shipped 392 releases with go-lives in every month and took four flagship programmes live.

Beyond my own function, I contribute the integration and architecture position to the bank’s strategic reviews, shape how integration is sourced, and set the standards that the bank and its vendors build to.
