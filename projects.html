Enterprise Platform Transformation

The bank’s growth was constrained by an integration foundation operating as a cost centre, not a capability. I repositioned integration as a strategic platform — replacing fragmented point-to-point connections with a unified, governed enterprise ecosystem led by a team of 40+.

Time-to-market for new products accelerated materially. Over fifty enterprise systems were unified under reusable, governed services. Partner onboarding shifted from months to days, and every platform migration was completed with zero customer-facing downtime.

Risk & Resilience

Real-Time Transaction Monitoring

Fraud detection operated on overnight batch cycles — by the time anomalies surfaced, losses were already realized. I led the redesign to intercept and evaluate transactions in real time, materially reducing the bank’s exposure without impacting payment latency.

The institution moved from reactive investigation to real-time prevention — satisfying SAMA’s expectations on monitoring while protecting customer trust and controlling financial exposure.

Regulatory Strategy

Open Banking & Ecosystem Strategy

SAMA mandated open banking across Saudi banks. Most treated it as a compliance checkbox. I positioned the program as the foundation for ecosystem banking — the same regulated APIs that satisfy SAMA also enable fintech partnerships, Banking-as-a-Service, and embedded finance.

The bank achieved SAMA-aligned compliance while building the partner governance, consent management, and operating readiness required for ecosystem-scale operations.

Institutional significance Regulatory compliance became a competitive advantage. The bank is now positioned as a platform for third-party innovation — with a BaaS foundation institutions that treated open banking as a checkbox do not have.
