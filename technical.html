From leadership to implementation

Why an executive keeps a lab

My executive work is about institutions, decisions and people: which capabilities a bank builds, how delivery is governed, which risks are accepted and who carries them. Those decisions only hold if the engineering underneath them is sound. The Lab is that engineering layer, written down — how the patterns behind integration platforms, open banking and resilient payments actually work, and the trade-offs an architect has to make to run them in production.
