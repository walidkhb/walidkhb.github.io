Decision briefs

Most architecture choices in a bank are settled in an engineering meeting. Each brief takes one of them — a caching strategy, a gateway, a replication topology — and asks what belongs above that meeting: who owns it, what risk the bank is accepting, what evidence a regulator will ask for, and what the team needs from leadership. The titles follow one formula on purpose: they name the pattern, then the decision it really is. Each brief links to a companion Lab article with the engineering detail.
