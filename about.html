About

Who I Am

A technology leader and systems architect who builds the platforms, APIs, and distributed systems that power modern financial institutions.

My work is centered around integration—not just as a technical layer, but as a core engineering discipline that defines how systems communicate, how data flows in real-time, and how organizations scale their digital capabilities without accumulating technical debt.

I think in systems. I build platforms. I write architecture that ships.

What I Lead

As Vice President, Enterprise Integration at Saudi Investment Bank—and recognized as a Tech100 Strategic Leader of the Future (Saudi Arabia Edition)—I own the design and execution of integration capabilities across critical banking platforms, directly supporting the bank's digital transformation agenda and Vision 2030 alignment.

This includes:

  • Enterprise integration architecture (ESB, iPaaS, message brokers)
  • API strategy, gateway management, and developer experience
  • Real-time event streaming and async messaging (Kafka, IBM MQ)
  • Open Banking API compliance (SAMA framework)
  • Cloud-native modernization and platform engineering
  • Cross-domain system interoperability (core banking, payments, cards, digital channels)

Integration in this context is not a middleware concern—it is the digital nervous system of the entire bank.

How I Think

I focus on structure before speed. Engineering rigor before shortcuts.

Most organizations struggle not because of lack of effort, but because of architectural fragmentation—inconsistent APIs, point-to-point integrations, no observability, unclear data ownership, and zero governance automation.

My approach:

Define clear domain boundaries and service contracts.

Enforce standards through automated pipelines, not documents.

Build reusable platform capabilities, not one-off integrations.

Instrument everything—observability is not optional.

Reduce complexity through architecture, not more tooling.

What I Build

  • API ecosystems with full lifecycle governance (design, publish, monitor, deprecate)
  • Event-driven architectures using Kafka, CQRS, and event sourcing patterns
  • Cloud-native microservices on containerized infrastructure

The goal is always the same: build systems that can evolve without breaking—and that other engineers want to build on.

Leadership Approach

I operate close to execution. I read code. I review PRs. I challenge designs in architecture boards.

I don't separate architecture from delivery or strategy from implementation. Real impact comes from connecting all three—and from leading engineers who build production systems, not slide decks.

I lead teams by:

Setting clear technical direction and architectural guardrails.

Challenging weak designs before they hit production.

Removing ambiguity through ADRs, service contracts, and runbooks.

Measuring outcomes—uptime, latency, throughput, mean time to recovery.

Building ownership and engineering culture, not just output.
